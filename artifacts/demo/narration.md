# NurseBridge demo narration

Visuals show a synthetic local browser run. The intake captions in this video come from the explicitly labeled mock transcript replay; they are not a recording of live speech recognition. The browser-to-browser nurse handoff was exercised with synthetic microphones in automated acceptance testing.
